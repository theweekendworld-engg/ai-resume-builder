# End-to-end QA, 2026-11-07 (local, as a new customer)

Walked as the owner: landing → free check (his resume_ai_voice.pdf + a recruiter
post) → sign-up with the stash → editor → chat paste of the post → Scout →
tailor → final PDF → Jobs, Job watch, Home, Goals, Radar, Work Log, Settings.
Telegram is tested after deploy.

## What works well
- Free check: requirement-level match against the employer ("47/100 … adding
  keywords cannot move this"), Public Trust flagged, rewrites grounded in the
  resume. The stash survives sign-up ("No need to upload it again").
- Scout: JD extraction accurate (must/nice split, pay as quoted), company facts
  cited and disagreements stated, LinkedIn search links instead of automation.
- Tailor asks before claiming a skill ("hands-on experience with MCP?").
- Home, Jobs, Job watch and Settings are clear and well organised.
